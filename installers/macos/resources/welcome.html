Faro accountability

Faro looks at what is on this computer's screen, detects explicit content on the computer itself, and sends the allies you chose an encrypted report with heavily blurred thumbnails. Allies are also told if monitoring stops, is tampered with, or has its settings changed.

Every account on this computer will be monitored, including accounts created later, guest sessions and users switched to with fast user switching. Switching accounts is not a way around Faro, and there is no per-account exception.

Everyone who logs in to this computer is told that it is monitored. If other people use it, tell them before you install.

Before you continue:

  • Turn off the guest account if it is on (System Settings > Users & Groups), so nobody can use the computer without an account.
  • Use a standard (non-admin) account day to day, and consider letting an ally keep the admin password.

When you enroll this computer, Faro lists its accounts so you can check who will be monitored.
